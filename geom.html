About GEOM

Learn. Create. Transform spaces.

GEOM Interior Design Masterclass is designed for aspiring interior designers, creative professionals and anyone who wants to understand how to transform ordinary spaces into beautiful and functional environments.

What You'll Learn

Practical skills to help you become a better designer.

Interior Design Principles

Understand balance, proportion, rhythm, harmony and functionality in interior spaces.

Space Planning

Learn how to organize furniture, movement and different areas within a space.

Colors & Materials

Discover how colors, textures and materials influence the atmosphere of an interior.

Lighting Design

Learn how to use natural and artificial lighting to enhance interior spaces.

Furniture Selection

Understand how to choose furniture based on style, comfort, scale and functionality.

Design Presentation

Learn how to present your ideas professionally to clients and communicate your design concepts.
